## New calendar primary evidence
CEE-AC-26-149 (27 Aug 2026) is a three-page certification of disagreement. The index title and the original page 1 both say "CALENDARIO ELECTORAL 2028". OCR of all three original pages confirms the draft calendar recommended 27 September 2028 and the PNP proposed 20 September 2028; PPD and PIP stated separate objections. There is no 2026-vs-2028 title contradiction. The certification is not itself the complete calendar.

CEE-RS-26-010 is an eight-page signed resolution dated 8 September 2026 that resolves the disagreement. It sets advance-vote-by-mail dispatch to start as soon as ballots are available but no later than 20 September 2028 and finish no later than 3 October 2028. The application window is 1 June 2027 through 18 September 2028. It distinguishes the 45-day UOCAVA federal ballot-transmission rule from the 50-day domestic advance-vote application deadline. The 23 September 2028 UOCAVA date is a computed date (45 days before 7 November), not the domestic application deadline.

The CEE RFP independently states primary date 6 June 2028 and general election 7 November 2028. Seven event rows are presently identified; full calendar event and amendment denominators remain UNKNOWN. Do not claim full-calendar closure.

## eRE/eJAVAA source distinctions
CEE-AC-26-009 (6 February 2025) created a special committee of three representatives per party with 60 days to evaluate eRE, eJAVAA and SIEN. CEE-AC-25-031 (1 May 2025) records unanimous adoption of the committee reports and directs OSIPE to have the relevant developers prepare new proposals incorporating those reports. The one-page adoption agreement does not attach the committee reports.

CEE-AC-26-110 (one page; issued 30 June 2026; meeting 24 June) states that the OSIPE eRE/eJAVAA test report was presented for approval, that the Commission unanimously approved it, and directs OSIPE to take steps toward putting the new version into operation, subject to the work schedule requested by the PPD commissioner. The one-page agreement is not the underlying report, test annex, tested build ID, implementation schedule, deployment receipt or independent security report.

CEE-AC-25-112 (21-page public CEE agreement/proposal; 29 October 2025) approves a contractor proposal and schedule for eRE improvements. Its proposed eRE schedule runs 5 January–2 June 2026: development 7 January–14 May; test environment 15–21 May; tests 22–28 May; acceptance 29 May; quality-control induction 1 June; eRE release scheduled 2 June. This is an eRE improvement schedule. Although the proposal contents include a separate eJAVAA Phase 2 scope, the eRE schedule must not be represented as an eJAVAA implementation schedule or as proof the scheduled eRE release occurred.

CEE-AC-25-031 (committee evaluation reports) and CEE-AC-25-009 (special committee creation) are listed on the official CEE agreement index but their PDF cache was unavailable in this research pass. CEE-AC-26-113 approves minutes for 24 June and 2 July 2026; the one-page approval does not include the minutes themselves. These are priority original records.
